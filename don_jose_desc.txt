Origin: Sierra de Candelario, Salamanca Located in the heart of Spain, the Sierra de Candelario region in Salamanca is considered the paradise of Iberian ham. The pristine environment, rich flora, and clean air create the perfect conditions for raising pure Iberian pigs. These pigs roam freely on the grasslands and feed on acorns that give the ham a distinctive nutty flavour. The traditional curing process, combined with the unique microclimate of the Sierra de Candelario, makes Don José Iberico Bellota Ham stand out in taste and quality. Features: 100% Iberico pigs: Raised in freedom in the pristine environment of Sierra de Candelario and fed on a diet of acorns. Aged for 36 months: Ensures a deep, rich flavour and perfect texture.
